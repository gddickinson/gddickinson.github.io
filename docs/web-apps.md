# Live Web Apps

Projects you can use straight from the browser. Each one also runs as a local web app with the full Python back end
(see each repository's README, "Web" section).

## Simulations and art

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **[Lava Lamp](https://gddickinson.github.io/lava_lamp/)**

    ---

    A physics-based lava lamp: heating, buoyancy and metaball rendering, with twelve colour schemes and wax types.
    Drag to stir.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/lava_lamp)

-   **[Chladni Visualizer](https://gddickinson.github.io/chladni_visualiser/)**

    ---

    Sand settling on the nodal lines of a vibrating plate: nine pattern families, mode numbers and musical-note
    presets.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/chladni_visualiser)

-   **[CALab](https://gddickinson.github.io/CALab/)**

    ---

    A cellular-automata lab with 13 automata, from Game of Life and WireWorld to Langton's Loop and von Neumann's
    self-reproducing rules.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/CALab)

</div>

## Analysis tools

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **[Direction Autocorrelation](https://gddickinson.github.io/direction_autocorrelation_analysis/)**

    ---

    Directional persistence of cell and particle trajectories. Load a CSV and the analysis runs in your browser.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/direction_autocorrelation_analysis)

-   **[Particle Trajectory Analyzer](https://gddickinson.github.io/particle_trajectory_analyzer/)**

    ---

    Radius of gyration, linearity and mobility classification of single-particle tracks, with real and simulated
    demo data.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/particle_trajectory_analyzer)

-   **[Microscope Atlas](https://gddickinson.github.io/microscope-atlas/)**

    ---

    How every major kind of microscope works, interactive physics simulations, and a sourced database of systems and
    fluorophores.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/microscope-atlas)

</div>
