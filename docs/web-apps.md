# Live Web Apps

Projects you can use straight from the browser. Each one also runs as a local web app with the full Python back end
(see each repository's README, "Web" section).

## Simulations and art

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **[Lava Lamp](https://gddickinson.github.io/lava_lamp/)**

    ---

    A physics-based lava lamp: heating, buoyancy and metaball rendering, with twelve colour schemes and wax types.
    Drag to stir.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/lava_lamp)

-   **[Chladni Visualizer](https://gddickinson.github.io/chladni_visualiser/)**

    ---

    Sand settling on the nodal lines of a vibrating plate: nine pattern families, mode numbers and musical-note
    presets.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/chladni_visualiser)

-   **[CALab](https://gddickinson.github.io/CALab/)**

    ---

    A cellular-automata lab with 13 automata, from Game of Life and WireWorld to Langton's Loop and von Neumann's
    self-reproducing rules.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/CALab)

-   **[Candle Flame](https://gddickinson.github.io/candle_flame/)**

    ---

    A real-time particle candle flame in WebGL: wind, flicker, wax colours, blow it out and relight it.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/candle_flame)

-   **[Suminagashi](https://gddickinson.github.io/suminagashi/)**

    ---

    Japanese ink marbling: drop ink, comb and swirl it with five tools and ten palettes, then save the print.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/suminagashi)

-   **[Fireworks](https://gddickinson.github.io/fireworks/)**

    ---

    A firework display with burst patterns, scenes and weather, all running in the browser.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/fireworks)

-   **[Kaleidoscope](https://gddickinson.github.io/kaleidoscope/)**

    ---

    A music-reactive kaleidoscope driven by an audio file, the microphone or a built-in demo signal.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/kaleidoscope)

-   **[Self-Replicating Automata](https://gddickinson.github.io/self_replicating_automata/)**

    ---

    Self-reproducing cellular automata after von Neumann: Langton's loop, Brian's Brain, WireWorld and more.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/self_replicating_automata)

-   **[Rhythmograms](https://gddickinson.github.io/rhythmograms/)**

    ---

    Heinrich Heidersberger's light-trace photographs recreated with four damped pendulums: 35 presets, morphing, PNG and SVG export.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/rhythmograms)

-   **[Wave Interference](https://gddickinson.github.io/interference_simulation/)**

    ---

    Waves in a ripple tank: slits, point sources, obstacles and standing waves, simulated live in the browser.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/interference_simulation)

-   **[Complex Functions](https://gddickinson.github.io/complex-function-visualization/)**

    ---

    Domain colouring and animations of complex functions, computed in the browser.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/complex-function-visualization)

-   **[Tile Filling](https://gddickinson.github.io/tile-filling/)**

    ---

    Twenty-two geometric tiling and space-filling patterns with palettes, effects and kaleidoscope, exported as PNG.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/tile-filling)

-   **[Optical Illusions](https://gddickinson.github.io/optical_illusion_pattern_generator/)**

    ---

    Classic optical illusions and mathematical patterns: 49 patterns from nine generators, drawn in the browser.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/optical_illusion_pattern_generator)

-   **[Organic Growth](https://gddickinson.github.io/organic-growth-patterns/)**

    ---

    Coral, lichen, mycelium and crystal growth as 2D and 3D cellular automata, grown in the browser.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/organic-growth-patterns)

-   **[Dot Matrix Art](https://gddickinson.github.io/dotMatrix_art/)**

    ---

    Turn your own photo into dot-matrix art with filters and palettes, then save it as PNG or SVG. The photo never leaves your browser.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/dotMatrix_art)

-   **[Colour Palette](https://gddickinson.github.io/colour-palette/)**

    ---

    ---

    Convert your own image to ASCII art with character sets, inversion and dithering; copy or download it. The image stays in your browser.

-   **[Wireframe Display](https://gddickinson.github.io/wireframe_display/)**

    ---

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/wireframe_display)

-   **[Wireframe Scenes](https://gddickinson.github.io/wireframeDisplay/)**

    ---

    A 3D wireframe scene viewer with a perspective camera, solids, knots and fractals.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/wireframeDisplay)

-   **[Landscape Generator](https://gddickinson.github.io/landscape-generator/)**

## Analysis tools

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **[Direction Autocorrelation](https://gddickinson.github.io/direction_autocorrelation_analysis/)**

    ---

    Directional persistence of cell and particle trajectories. Load a CSV and the analysis runs in your browser.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/direction_autocorrelation_analysis)

-   **[Particle Trajectory Analyzer](https://gddickinson.github.io/particle_trajectory_analyzer/)**

    ---

    Radius of gyration, linearity and mobility classification of single-particle tracks, with real and simulated
    demo data.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/particle_trajectory_analyzer)

-   **[Microscope Atlas](https://gddickinson.github.io/microscope-atlas/)**

    ---

    How every major kind of microscope works, interactive physics simulations, and a sourced database of systems and
    fluorophores.

    [:octicons-mark-github-16: Source](https://github.com/gddickinson/microscope-atlas)

</div>
